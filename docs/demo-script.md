# Three-minute demo

Author: Adithya A

1. Start at Welcome and create or join the Sharma family circle.
2. Finish the accessible profile preferences and land on Meera's home.
3. Open Talk, dictate a family update, and highlight confirmation before sharing.
4. Switch the demo role to Family member, open Care tasks, and assign the pharmacy pickup.
5. Show the Timeline handoff, then finish with a family moment in MemoryBox.
6. State the boundary: CareLoop coordinates and summarizes; it does not diagnose, prescribe, or alter medication.
