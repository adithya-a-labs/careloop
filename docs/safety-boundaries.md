# Safety boundaries

Author: Adithya A

CareLoop coordinates and summarizes. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or alter medication.

- Never represent a wellbeing check-in as a clinical assessment.
- Refuse diagnosis, dose changes, stopping medication or prescribing requests.
- For urgent or dangerous symptoms, direct the person to local emergency services or a qualified clinician; do not improvise care instructions.
- Require review before sharing generated summaries or committing voice-created actions.
- Preserve source attribution and timestamps in summaries.
- Minimize sensitive context and enforce circle membership on every data operation.
